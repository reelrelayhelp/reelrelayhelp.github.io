Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

This Privacy Policy explains how Reelrelay ("we," "us," "our") handles information in connection with our content-publishing tool (the "Service"), which schedules and posts short-form videos that we own to our own connected social media accounts.

Who we are

The Service is operated by Reelrelay. You can reach us at reelrelay.help@gmail.com.

What the Service does

The Service publishes video content that we have created and own to social media accounts that we control. It does not offer accounts to the public and does not collect or process the personal data of third-party end users.

Information we access through connected platforms

When we connect one of our own social media accounts (for example, via TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API), the platform provides limited information needed to publish on that account, which may include: an access token and refresh token that authorize posting to that account; basic account/creator information returned by the platform (such as the account nickname and the posting options available to that account); and the status of posts we submit. We request only the permissions required to publish our own content (for example, the TikTok video.publish scope).

How we use this information

We use it solely to authenticate to the connected account, upload and publish our own videos to that account, and confirm whether a post succeeded. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell or share it.

How we store and protect it

Access and refresh tokens are stored privately in our own environment configuration and are not exposed publicly. Tokens are used only to call the connected platform's publishing endpoints and are retained only while an account remains connected.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties, except with the connected platform itself (for example, TikTok) as required to publish content, and where required by law.

Data retention and deletion

We keep connection tokens only for as long as the account stays connected to the Service. You can disconnect an account at any time from the connected platform's settings or by contacting us at reelrelay.help@gmail.com, after which we delete the stored tokens for that account. To request deletion of any information we hold, email reelrelay.help@gmail.com.

Platform policies

Our use of TikTok data complies with the TikTok Developer Terms of Service and TikTok's Content Sharing and Developer policies. Our use and transfer of information received from platform APIs adhere to those platforms' API Services policies, including their Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Children

The Service is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: reelrelay.help@gmail.com.
