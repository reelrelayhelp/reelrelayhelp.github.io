A simple scheduling tool for our own short-form videos.

Reelrelay schedules and publishes short educational videos that we create and own to our own connected social media accounts, so our publishing stays consistent across platforms.

What it does

The tool connects to social accounts we own (via each platform's official login), re-posts videos we have already produced, and confirms whether each post succeeded. It does not offer accounts to the public and does not process other people's personal data.

How your data is handled

We request only the permission needed to publish our own content on our own accounts, and we store the connection securely. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
